LGPL source/relinking
--------------------
FFmpeg and libmpv are shipped as separate programs / shared libraries and are
not linked into the Highlight Creator application code. Anyone redistributing a
build must keep the LGPL license texts above and make the corresponding source
available. The exact fetch/build commands are in fetch_lgpl_deps.ps1.
